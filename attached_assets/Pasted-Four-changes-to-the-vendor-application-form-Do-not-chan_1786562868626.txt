Four changes to the vendor application form. Do not change anything else.

1. SPACE SIZES. The "Number of spaces requested" options must show the physical space included, which differs by category:

Major Food Vendor — Single: 10'x20' — Double: 20'x20'
Specialty Food & Beverage — Single: 10'x10' — Double: 10'x20'
Retail, Artisan & Business — Single: 10'x10' — Double: 10'x20'
Verified Nonprofit — Single: 10'x10' — Double: 10'x20'

Keep the existing price display (single = category fee, double = 2x fee) and show the dimensions alongside it, updating as the selected category changes.

2. SETUP TYPE. Remove "Food Trailer" and "Food Truck" from "What type of setup will you have?" — only "Standard 10'x10' Tent" and "Other (describe)" remain. Food trucks and trailers are not permitted at this festival.

3. FIRE MARSHAL NOTE. Replace the note under that question with:

"Only 10'x10' pop-up tents are permitted. Food trucks and food trailers are not allowed. Major Food Vendors receive a 10'x20' footprint, which may be used as two 10'x10' tents or a single tent within the larger space. Any tent larger than 10'x10' must be approved by the Roseville Fire Marshal, and it is the vendor's responsibility to obtain that approval."

Add this required acknowledgement to the vendor agreement section:

"I understand that food trucks and food trailers are not permitted, and that any tent larger than 10'x10' requires approval from the Roseville Fire Marshal, which I am responsible for obtaining."

4. FOOD AND COOKING QUESTIONS. These should appear ONLY for Major Food Vendor and Specialty Food & Beverage Vendor. Verified Nonprofit Organizations and Retail, Artisan & Business vendors must not see any of them:

- Will you be preparing food on-site?
- Will you be using propane? (and its tank count / tank size follow-ups)
- Cooking equipment checkboxes
- The Health Permit section, including the Placer County TFF link and the health department acknowledgement checkbox

Also add "None" as the first option in the Cooking equipment checkboxes: None, Grill, Flat Top, Fryer, Smoker, Generator, Other. Selecting "None" clears and disables the others; selecting any other option clears "None".
