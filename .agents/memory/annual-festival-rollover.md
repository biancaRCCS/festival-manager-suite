---
name: Annual festival rollover
description: Preserve historical festival records and avoid carrying forward unconfirmed operational dates and materials when changing years.
---

Create a separate active festival year rather than changing the year or event date on the previous year's record. Leave prior-year applications and payment history associated with their original year. Do not silently transplant prior-year application, document, or artwork deadlines or publish the previous year's guide as current.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose activating 2027 while preserving 2026 records. Deadlines and guides are distinct operational commitments, not consequences of the new event date.

**How to apply:** For future rollovers, configure the new year's event date independently; leave unspecified deadlines unset and make outdated guide links unavailable on current-year application pages until replacements are supplied. Verify the active year separately in development and the published app, as publishing code does not copy development data to production.
